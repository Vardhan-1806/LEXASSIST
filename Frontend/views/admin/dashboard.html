🗺️ Business Roadmap

● LIVE NOW

Phase 1 — Foundation (2024)

Free platform for all citizens and lawyers. Core features: AI chatbot powered by TF-IDF RAG on Indian legal database, lawyer verification by admin, case management with timeline tracking, court appointment booking, document generation (FIR, Notice, Bail), email notifications via Gmail SMTP, individual calendars with hearing reminders.

Activate subscription plans: Citizen Pro ₹49/month, Lawyer Pro ₹199/month. 10-15% platform commission on court appointments. Document credit packs. SMS hearing reminders. Razorpay payment integration. Advanced AI with GPT upgrade.
