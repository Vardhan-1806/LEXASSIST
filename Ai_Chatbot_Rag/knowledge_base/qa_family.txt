LEXASSIST LEGAL QA — FAMILY LAW

Q: What was the primary issue in the case Anu Bhandari vs. Pradip Bhandari?
A: The primary issue was the settlement of all disputes and litigations between Anu Bhandari and Pradip Bhandari, including the dissolution of their marriage by mutual consent.
Case: Anu Bhandari vs. Pradip Bhandari
Date: 5th March 2018

---

Q: What were the main grounds for divorce in the case of Smt. Roopa Soni vs. Kamalnarayan Soni?
A: The main grounds for divorce were cruelty and desertion as per Section 13(1) of the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955.
Case: Smt. Roopa Soni vs. Kamalnarayan Soni

---

Q: What was the significance of the Amending Act of 1976 in the context of divorce under the Hindu Marriage Act?
A: The Amending Act of 1976 liberalized the provisions for granting divorce by introducing clauses (ia) and (ib) to Section 13, facilitating the expeditious disposal of proceedings and removing certain anomalies.
Case: Smt. Roopa Soni vs. Kamalnarayan Soni

---

Case: Kanta Yadav vs. Om Prakash Yadav & Ors.
