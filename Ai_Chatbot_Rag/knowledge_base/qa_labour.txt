LEXASSIST LEGAL QA — LABOUR LAW
============================================================

Q: What was the main issue in the case Union of India vs. Maj. Gen. Manomoy Ganguly?
A: The main issue was Maj. Gen. Manomoy Ganguly's denial of promotion to the position of Director General Medical Services (Army) despite being eligible and senior.
Case: Union of India vs. Maj. Gen. Manomoy Ganguly
Date: 1st August 2018

---

Q: What decision did the Armed Forces Tribunal (AFT) make regarding Maj. Gen. Manomoy Ganguly's promotion?
A: The AFT directed the appellants to post Maj. Gen. Manomoy Ganguly as DGMS (Army) as expeditiously as possible and within one month from the date of the judgment.
Case: Union of India vs. Maj. Gen. Manomoy Ganguly
Date: 1st August 2018

---

Q: What was the conclusion of the Customs, Excise and Service Tax Appellate Tribunal (CESTAT) regarding the product's classification?
A: The CESTAT concluded that the product was not a heat pump and therefore ineligible for the concessional rate of duty under Sl. No. 2 of Notification No. 155/86-CE dated 1.3.1986. The product was declared a complete machine, not part of a machine, and the value of Lithium Bromide was not to be included in the assessable value of the machine.
Case: M/s Thermax Ltd. vs. Commissioner of Central Excise, Pune-1
Date: 13th October 2022

---

Q: What was the primary legal issue in the case Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Limited vs. The Board of Trustees of Jawaharlal Nehru Port Authority & Ors.?
A: The primary legal issue was whether the disqualification of Adani Ports from participating in two tenders issued by Jawaharlal Nehru Port Authority (JNPA) due to the termination of a Concession Agreement by Visakhapatnam Port Authority was justified.
Case: Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Limited vs. The Board of Trustees of Jawaharlal Nehru Port Authority & Ors.
Date: 5th September 2022

---

Q: What was Adani Ports' argument regarding the termination of the Concession Agreement?
A: Adani Ports argued that the termination of the Concession Agreement by Visakhapatnam Port Authority was a counterblast to their earlier termination of the same agreement, and that the issue of termination was still pending before the Arbitral Tribunal.
Case: Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Limited vs. The Board of Trustees of Jawaharlal Nehru Port Authority & Ors.
Date: 5th September 2022

---

Q: What consensus was reached between Adani Ports and the respondents?
A: A broad consensus was reached that the termination of the Concession Agreement by Visakhapatnam Port Authority would not be treated as a disqualification for Adani Ports in future tenders, and Adani Ports agreed not to claim any right to participate in the two specific tenders in question.
Case: Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Limited vs. The Board of Trustees of Jawaharlal Nehru Port Authority & Ors.
Date: 5th September 2022

---

Q: What was the State of Kerala's position on implementing the UGC Regulations regarding the retirement age?
A: The State of Kerala adopted the UGC Regulations but did not enhance the retirement age of university teachers to 65 years, citing its discretion to decide on service conditions for its employees.
Case: Dr. J. Vijayan & Others vs. The State of Kerala & Others
Date: 2nd August 2022

---

Q: How did the assessee treat the charges for containers and services provided to customers?
A: The assessee treated the charges for containers and services provided to customers as income from ancillary or allied ventures and did not include them in the value for the purpose of excise duty.
Case: Commissioner of Central Excise, Indore vs. M/S Grasim Industries Ltd.
Date: 11th May 2018

Q: What was the primary issue addressed in the case Indian Ex Servicemen Movement & Ors. vs Union of India & Ors.?

Case: Indian Ex Servicemen Movement & Ors. vs Union of India & Ors.

Q: What recommendations did the Koshyari Committee make regarding OROP?
A: The Koshyari Committee recommended the implementation of OROP, defining it as a uniform pension for armed forces personnel retiring in the same rank with the same length of service, irrespective of their retirement date, with future pension enhancements automatically passed on to past pensioners.
Case: Indian Ex Servicemen Movement & Ors. vs Union of India & Ors.
Date: 16th March 2022

Q: What is the rule regarding the 25% vacancies for the Postman position after the amendment on 30th January 1995?
A: After the amendment on 30th January 1995, 25% of the vacancies for the Postman position are to be filled from amongst extra-departmental Agents with a minimum of 15 years of service based on their seniority. If there are not enough candidates, the vacancies are to be filled by direct recruits through an examination, where the cut-off date is applicable.
Case: M. Elangovan vs. Union of India & Ors.
Date: 17th July 2017

Q: What was the policy framed by the Central Government regarding government accommodation for retired Kashmiri migrants?
A: The policy, framed on 28th March 2017 and modified on 19th May 2017, provided alternate residence to retired Central Government employees belonging to the State of Jammu & Kashmir who possess General Pool Residential Accommodation (GPRA) in Delhi.
Case: Union of India & Anr. vs. Omkar Nath Dhar (D) Through L.Rs.

---

Q: What were the terms and conditions under the policy for qualifying as a 'Kashmiri migrant'?
A: The applicant must be a retired Central Government employee or their spouse, possess GPRA in Delhi, be a permanent resident of Jammu and Kashmir, have no other residence in the country, and not be in a position to return to their native place due to unfavorable conditions.
Case: Union of India & Anr. vs. Omkar Nath Dhar (D) Through L.Rs.

Q: On what grounds did the Union of India appeal against the Tribunal's decision?

Case: Union of India & Ors. vs. Ex. Naik Ram Singh

---

Q: What is Regulation 173 of the Pension Regulations for the Army, 1961?
A: Regulation 173 states that a disability pension may be granted to an individual who is invalidated out of service on account of disability which is attributable to or aggravated by military service in non-battle casualty and is assessed at 20% or over.
Case: Union of India & Ors. vs. Ex. Naik Ram Singh
