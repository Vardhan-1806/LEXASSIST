LEXASSIST — IMPORTANT INDIAN LAWS FAQ
POCSO, Domestic Violence, Motor Vehicle, Divorce, Property, Labour
============================================================

Q: What is POCSO Act?
A: POCSO (Protection of Children from Sexual Offences) Act 2012 protects children below 18 from sexual abuse. Key provisions: Penetrative sexual assault — minimum 10 years, may extend to life. Aggravated penetrative sexual assault — minimum 20 years. Sexual harassment of child — up to 3 years. Child pornography — up to 5 years. Special courts handle POCSO cases. Complaint can be made to police, SJPU (Special Juvenile Police Unit), or CHILDLINE (1098).

Q: What is domestic violence law in India?
A: Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act 2005 covers: Physical abuse, Sexual abuse, Emotional/verbal abuse, Economic abuse. WHO CAN FILE: Wife, live-in partner, daughter, mother, sister. HOW TO FILE: Approach Protection Officer (PO) in your district, or file directly in Magistrate court. RELIEF available: Protection order — stops abuser from entering home, Residence order — right to stay in shared home, Monetary relief — maintenance and compensation, Custody order — children's custody. HELPLINE: 181 (Women Helpline), 1091

Q: What is Section 498A IPC?
A: Section 498A IPC deals with cruelty by husband or his relatives towards wife. Punishment: Up to 3 years imprisonment and fine. Cruelty means: 1. Wilful conduct likely to drive woman to suicide or cause danger to life, limb or health 2. Harassment to coerce her or her relatives to meet unlawful demand for property/dowry. This is a non-bailable, cognizable offence. To file: Go to police station and file FIR, or approach Magistrate directly under Section 200 CrPC.

Q: What is Motor Vehicle Act? What to do after accident?
A: Motor Vehicle Act 1988 (Amended 2019) governs traffic and accidents. After an accident:
1. Call police (100) and ambulance (108) immediately
2. Don't move seriously injured people
3. Note vehicle number, driver details, witnesses
4. File FIR at nearest police station within 24 hours
5. Claim compensation from Motor Accident Claims Tribunal (MACT)

Compensation: Third party insurance is mandatory. No-fault compensation (hit and run): Up to ₹2 lakh (death), ₹50,000 (grievous hurt). New penalties 2019: Drunk driving — ₹10,000 or 6 months jail. Overspeeding — ₹1,000-₹2,000.

Q: How to file for divorce in India?
A: MUTUAL CONSENT DIVORCE (Faster — 6-18 months):
1. Both agree to divorce
2. Must have lived separately for at least 1 year
3. File joint petition in Family Court
4. First motion hearing
5. 6 months cooling period (court may waive)
6. Second motion — divorce granted

CONTESTED DIVORCE (Slower — 2-5 years):
Grounds: Cruelty, Desertion (2+ years), Adultery, Conversion, Mental disorder, Leprosy
File petition → notices sent → response → evidence → arguments → judgment

Different personal laws apply: Hindu Marriage Act (Hindus, Sikhs, Buddhists, Jains), Muslim Personal Law, Indian Divorce Act (Christians), Special Marriage Act

Q: What is property registration? Is it mandatory?
A: Under Registration Act 1908, all immovable property transactions above ₹100 MUST be registered. If not registered, the document is not admissible as evidence in court. Steps to register property:
1. Prepare Sale Deed with all details
2. Get it verified by a lawyer
3. Pay Stamp Duty (3-10% of value, varies by state)
4. Pay Registration Fee (1% of value, max ₹30,000 in most states)
5. Visit Sub-Registrar office with seller and two witnesses
6. Biometric verification and photo
7. Registered document delivered within 7 working days

Daughters have equal rights in ancestral property (Supreme Court 2020 judgment in Vineeta Sharma case).

Q: What is succession law in India? Who inherits property?
A: HINDU SUCCESSION ACT 1956 (for Hindus, Sikhs, Buddhists, Jains):
Class I heirs (get first priority): Son, daughter, widow, mother, son's son, son's daughter, son's widow, daughter's son, daughter's daughter.
Daughters have EQUAL rights as sons in ancestral property since 2005 amendment.

MUSLIM PERSONAL LAW (for Muslims):
Female heirs get half the share of male heirs.

INDIAN SUCCESSION ACT 1925 (for Christians, Parsis):
Spouse gets 1/3, remaining goes to children.

If person dies WITHOUT WILL: Property goes to legal heirs as per above laws.
If person dies WITH WILL: Probate of will required from court.

Q: What is labour law in India? What are worker rights?
A: KEY LABOUR LAWS:
1. Minimum Wages Act 1948 — Every worker entitled to minimum wage fixed by state govt
2. Payment of Wages Act 1936 — Wages must be paid on time, without unauthorized deductions
3. Payment of Gratuity Act 1972 — After 5 years continuous service, gratuity = (Last salary × 15 × years)/26
4. EPF Act 1952 — Both employer and employee contribute 12% each to provident fund
5. Factories Act 1948 — Maximum 48 hours/week, overtime at double rate
6. Industrial Disputes Act 1947 — Protects against wrongful termination

WRONGFUL TERMINATION:
File complaint with Labour Commissioner → Labour Court → High Court
Online complaint: shramsuvidha.gov.in
