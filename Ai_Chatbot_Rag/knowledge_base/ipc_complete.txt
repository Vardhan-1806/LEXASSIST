LEXASSIST — COMPLETE INDIAN PENAL CODE REFERENCE
All major IPC sections with simple explanations
============================================================

Q: What is Section 299 IPC? What does IPC 299 say? Explain Section 299 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 299 IPC — Culpable homicide**
Whoever causes death by doing an act with the intention of causing death, or with the intention of causing such bodily injury as is likely to cause death, or with the knowledge that such act is likely to cause death, commits the offence of culpable homicide.
Section: 299
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Culpable homicide

---

Q: What is Section 300 IPC? What does IPC 300 say? Explain Section 300 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 300 IPC — Murder**
Culpable homicide is murder if done with intention to cause death. Punishment: Death or imprisonment for life, and fine.
Section: 300
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Murder

---

Q: What is Section 302 IPC? What does IPC 302 say? Explain Section 302 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 302 IPC — Punishment for murder**
Whoever commits murder shall be punished with death, or imprisonment for life, and shall also be liable to fine.
Section: 302
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Punishment for murder

---

Q: What is Section 304 IPC? What does IPC 304 say? Explain Section 304 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 304 IPC — Punishment for culpable homicide not amounting to murder**
Imprisonment for life or imprisonment for 10 years and fine, depending on whether the act was done with intention or knowledge.
Section: 304
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Punishment for culpable homicide not amounting to murder

---

Q: What is Section 304B IPC? What does IPC 304B say? Explain Section 304B of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 304B IPC — Dowry death**
Where the death of a woman is caused by any burns or bodily injury or occurs under suspicious circumstances within 7 years of marriage and it is shown that she was subjected to cruelty by her husband or relatives in connection with demand for dowry — imprisoned for 7 years minimum, may extend to life.
Section: 304B
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Dowry death

---

Q: What is Section 306 IPC? What does IPC 306 say? Explain Section 306 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 306 IPC — Abetment of suicide**
If any person commits suicide, whoever abets the commission of such suicide shall be punished with imprisonment up to 10 years and fine.
Section: 306
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Abetment of suicide

---

Q: What is Section 307 IPC? What does IPC 307 say? Explain Section 307 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 307 IPC — Attempt to murder**
Whoever does any act with such intention or knowledge and under such circumstances that if they caused death by that act, they would be guilty of murder — imprisonment up to 10 years and fine. If the act causes hurt — imprisonment for life or 10 years.
Section: 307
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Attempt to murder

---

Q: What is Section 308 IPC? What does IPC 308 say? Explain Section 308 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 308 IPC — Attempt to commit culpable homicide**
Whoever does any act with such intention or knowledge that if the act caused death, they would be guilty of culpable homicide not amounting to murder — imprisonment up to 3 years or fine or both. If hurt is caused — up to 7 years.
Section: 308
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Attempt to commit culpable homicide

---

Q: What is Section 319 IPC? What does IPC 319 say? Explain Section 319 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 319 IPC — Hurt**
Whoever causes bodily pain, disease or infirmity to any person is said to cause hurt.
Section: 319
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Hurt

---

Q: What is Section 320 IPC? What does IPC 320 say? Explain Section 320 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 320 IPC — Grievous hurt**
The following kinds of hurt only are designated as grievous: emasculation; permanent privation of sight or hearing; privation of any member or joint; destruction or permanent impairing of the powers of any member or joint; permanent disfiguration of the head or face; fracture or dislocation of a bone or tooth; any hurt which endangers life.
Section: 320
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Grievous hurt

---

Q: What is Section 323 IPC? What does IPC 323 say? Explain Section 323 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 323 IPC — Punishment for voluntarily causing hurt**
Whoever voluntarily causes hurt shall be punished with imprisonment up to one year or fine up to Rs.1000 or both.
Section: 323
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Punishment for voluntarily causing hurt

---

Q: What is Section 324 IPC? What does IPC 324 say? Explain Section 324 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 324 IPC — Voluntarily causing hurt by dangerous weapons**
Whoever voluntarily causes hurt by means of any instrument for shooting, stabbing or cutting or by fire or heated substance or by poison or by any animal — imprisonment up to 3 years or fine or both.
Section: 324
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Voluntarily causing hurt by dangerous weapons

---

Q: What is Section 354 IPC? What does IPC 354 say? Explain Section 354 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 354 IPC — Assault or criminal force to woman with intent to outrage her modesty**
Whoever assaults or uses criminal force to any woman intending to outrage or knowing it to be likely that he will thereby outrage her modesty — imprisonment of not less than 1 year up to 5 years and fine.
Section: 354
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Assault or criminal force to woman with intent to outrage her modesty

---

Q: What is Section 354A IPC? What does IPC 354A say? Explain Section 354A of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 354A IPC — Sexual harassment**
A man committing sexual harassment — making unwelcome sexual advances, physical contact, demanding sexual favors, showing pornography — shall be punished with rigorous imprisonment up to 3 years or fine or both.
Section: 354A
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Sexual harassment

---

Q: What is Section 354D IPC? What does IPC 354D say? Explain Section 354D of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 354D IPC — Stalking**
Any man who follows a woman and contacts or attempts to contact her to foster personal interaction repeatedly despite clear indication of disinterest, or monitors her use of internet, email, or any other form of electronic communication — imprisonment up to 3 years (first conviction), up to 5 years (second or subsequent conviction).
Section: 354D
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Stalking

---

Q: What is Section 363 IPC? What does IPC 363 say? Explain Section 363 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 363 IPC — Kidnapping**
Whoever kidnaps any person from India or from lawful guardianship shall be punished with imprisonment up to 7 years and fine.
Section: 363
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Kidnapping

---

Q: What is Section 375 IPC? What does IPC 375 say? Explain Section 375 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 375 IPC — Rape**
A man is said to commit rape if he has sexual intercourse with a woman against her will, without her consent, with her consent obtained by putting her in fear of death or hurt, with her consent when she is intoxicated, or with a woman under 18 years of age.
Section: 375
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Rape

---

Q: What is Section 376 IPC? What does IPC 376 say? Explain Section 376 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 376 IPC — Punishment for rape**
Whoever commits rape shall be punished with rigorous imprisonment for a term which shall not be less than 7 years but which may extend to imprisonment for life, and shall also be liable to fine. Aggravated rape: not less than 10 years.
Section: 376
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Punishment for rape

---

Q: What is Section 378 IPC? What does IPC 378 say? Explain Section 378 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 378 IPC — Theft**
Whoever intending to take dishonestly any moveable property out of the possession of any person without that person's consent moves that property in order to such taking, is said to commit theft.
Section: 378
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Theft

Q: What is Section 379 IPC? What does IPC 379 say? Explain Section 379 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 379 IPC — Punishment for theft**
Whoever commits theft shall be punished with imprisonment up to 3 years or fine or both.
Section: 379
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Punishment for theft

---

Q: What is Section 383 IPC? What does IPC 383 say? Explain Section 383 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 383 IPC — Extortion**
Whoever intentionally puts any person in fear of any injury to that person or another, and dishonestly induces the person so put in fear to deliver to any person any property or document, commits extortion.
Section: 383
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Extortion

---

Q: What is Section 390 IPC? What does IPC 390 say? Explain Section 390 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 390 IPC — Robbery**
In all robbery there is either theft or extortion. Theft is robbery if done with the use of force or causing fear of death or grievous hurt.
Section: 390
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Robbery

Q: What is Section 415 IPC? What does IPC 415 say? Explain Section 415 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 415 IPC — Cheating**
Whoever by deceiving any person fraudulently or dishonestly induces the person so deceived to deliver any property to any person, or to consent that any person shall retain any property, or intentionally induces the person so deceived to do or omit to do anything which they would not do or omit if they were not so deceived — commits cheating.
Section: 415
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Cheating

---

Q: What is Section 420 IPC? What does IPC 420 say? Explain Section 420 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 420 IPC — Cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property**
Whoever cheats and thereby dishonestly induces the person deceived to deliver any property to any person, or to make, alter or destroy the whole or any part of a valuable security — shall be punished with imprisonment up to 7 years and fine. This is a non-bailable, cognizable offence. Examples: online fraud, fake job offers, property fraud, selling fake products. To file a case: file FIR at police station, collect all evidence (messages, receipts, screenshots).
Section: 420
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property

---

Q: What is Section 425 IPC? What does IPC 425 say? Explain Section 425 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 425 IPC — Mischief**
Whoever with intent to cause or knowing that they are likely to cause wrongful loss or damage to the public or any person causes the destruction of any property, or any such change in any property or in the situation thereof as destroys or diminishes its value or utility — commits mischief.
Section: 425
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Mischief

Whoever enters into or upon property in the possession of another with intent to commit an offence or to intimidate, insult or annoy any person in possession of such property — commits criminal trespass.
Section: 441
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Criminal trespass

A: **Section 463 IPC — Forgery**
Whoever makes any false document or false electronic record or part of a document or electronic record with intent to cause damage or injury to the public or to any person, or to support any claim or title, or to cause any person to part with property — commits forgery.
Section: 463
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Forgery

---

Q: What is Section 498A IPC? What does IPC 498A say? Explain Section 498A of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 498A IPC — Husband or relative of husband of a woman subjecting her to cruelty**
Whoever, being the husband or the relative of the husband of a woman, subjects such woman to cruelty — shall be punished with imprisonment up to 3 years and fine. Cruelty means: wilful conduct likely to drive woman to suicide or cause danger to life, or harassment to coerce her to meet unlawful demand for property or dowry.
Section: 498A
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Husband or relative of husband of a woman subjecting her to cruelty

Whoever by words either spoken or intended to be read, or by signs or by visible representations, makes or publishes any imputation concerning any person intending to harm, or knowing or having reason to believe that such imputation will harm the reputation of such person — defames that person.
Section: 499
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Defamation

---

Whoever defames another shall be punished with simple imprisonment up to 2 years or fine or both.
Section: 500
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Punishment for defamation

A: **Section 504 IPC — Intentional insult with intent to provoke breach of peace**
Whoever intentionally insults and thereby gives provocation to any person, intending or knowing that such provocation will cause that person to break public peace — imprisonment up to 2 years or fine or both.
Section: 504
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Intentional insult with intent to provoke breach of peace

Q: What is Section 506 IPC? What does IPC 506 say? Explain Section 506 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 506 IPC — Punishment for criminal intimidation**
Whoever commits criminal intimidation shall be punished with imprisonment up to 2 years or fine or both. If threat is to cause death or grievous hurt or destruction of property by fire — up to 7 years.
Section: 506
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Punishment for criminal intimidation

---

Q: What is Section 509 IPC? What does IPC 509 say? Explain Section 509 of Indian Penal Code.
A: **Section 509 IPC — Word, gesture or act intended to insult the modesty of a woman**
Whoever intending to insult the modesty of any woman utters any word, makes any sound or gesture, or exhibits any object intending that such word or sound shall be heard by, or such gesture or object seen by such woman — imprisonment up to 3 years and fine.
Section: 509
Act: Indian Penal Code (IPC)
Title: Word, gesture or act intended to insult the modesty of a woman
