LEXASSIST LEGAL QA — PROPERTY LAW

Q: What was the main issue in the case Davesh Nagalya (D) & Ors. vs. Pradeep Kumar (D) Thr. LRs. and Ors.?
A: The main issue was whether the tenancy and partnership between Pradeep Kumar and Subhash Chand ended with the death of Pradeep Kumar, leading to the deemed vacancy of the property under Section 12(2) of the U.P. Urban Buildings (Regulation of Letting, Rent and Eviction) Act, 1972.
Case: Davesh Nagalya (D) & Ors. vs. Pradeep Kumar (D) Thr. LRs. and Ors.
Date: 10th August 2021

---

Q: What was the argument presented by the appellants regarding the partnership after the death of Pradeep Kumar in the case Davesh Nagalya (D) & Ors. vs. Pradeep Kumar (D) Thr. LRs. and Ors.?
A: The appellants argued that the partnership between Pradeep Kumar and Subhash Chand ended automatically with the death of Pradeep Kumar on 21st May 2004, and therefore, the tenancy also ended under Section 12(2) of the U.P. Urban Buildings (Regulation of Letting, Rent and Eviction) Act, 1972.
Case: Davesh Nagalya (D) & Ors. vs. Pradeep Kumar (D) Thr. LRs. and Ors.
Date: 10th August 2021

---

Q: What was the main dispute in the case Selvi vs. Gopalakrishnan Nair (D) Thr. LRs. and Ors.?
A: The main dispute was regarding the ownership and partition of the suit properties, particularly whether Survey No.988 was part of the properties to be partitioned.
Case: Selvi vs. Gopalakrishnan Nair (D) Thr. LRs. and Ors.
Date: 15th May 2018

---

Q: What was the decision of the trial court regarding the partition of the suit properties in the case Selvi vs. Gopalakrishnan Nair (D) Thr. LRs. and Ors.?
A: The trial court passed a preliminary decree for partition, holding that the plaintiffs were entitled to redeem and recover possession of 7.40 acres from the defendants by depositing the mortgage money.
Case: Selvi vs. Gopalakrishnan Nair (D) Thr. LRs. and Ors.
Date: 15th May 2018

Ltd., was not entitled to specific performance as the purchaser had failed to pay the balance sale consideration within the stipulated time and the suit was barred by limitation.

Q: What was the main issue in the case Mohd. Inam vs. Sanjay Kumar Singhal & Ors.?
A: The main issue was whether the tenancy of the suit premises was validly transferred to Mohd. Inam after the death of the original tenant, Rashid Ahmed, and whether the premises were rightly declared as vacant by the Rent Control and Eviction Officer.
Case: Mohd. Inam vs. Sanjay Kumar Singhal & Ors.
Date: 26th June 2020

---

Q: What was the claim made by the landlords, Sanjay Kumar Singhal & Ors., regarding the suit premises?
A: The landlords claimed that Rashid Ahmed had sub-let the suit premises to persons who were not family members, thereby violating the provisions of the U.P. Urban Buildings (Regulation of Letting, Rent and Eviction) Act, 1972.
Case: Mohd. Inam vs. Sanjay Kumar Singhal & Ors.
Date: 26th June 2020

---

Q: What was the conclusion of the Rent Control and Eviction Officer regarding the tenancy of the suit premises?
A: The Rent Control and Eviction Officer concluded that the suit premises were indeed sub-let to non-family members and declared the premises as vacant, allowing the landlords to take possession.
Case: Mohd. Inam vs. Sanjay Kumar Singhal & Ors.
Date: 26th June 2020

---

Q: What argument did Mohd. Inam present against the declaration of vacancy?
A: Mohd. Inam argued that he, along with his family members, was residing in the suit premises as lawful tenants and that there were no non-family members residing there.
Case: Mohd. Inam vs. Sanjay Kumar Singhal & Ors.
Date: 26th June 2020

Q: What were the grounds on which the respondent sought leave to defend in the eviction proceedings?
