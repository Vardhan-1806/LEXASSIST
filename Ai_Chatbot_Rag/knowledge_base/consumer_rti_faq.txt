LEXASSIST — CONSUMER RIGHTS AND RTI FAQ
============================================================

Q: How to file a consumer complaint?
A: **Filing Consumer Complaint (Step by Step)**
1. First send legal notice to company giving 15 days to respond
2. If no reply, file at District Consumer Commission (up to ₹1 crore)
3. Pay filing fee (₹100–₹5000)
4. File online at edaakhil.nic.in — completely free!
5. Attach: bill/receipt, warranty card, photos of defect, all communication
6. File within 2 years of the problem
7. What you can get: full refund, replacement, compensation for mental stress

---

Q: What is Consumer Protection Act 2019?
A: The Consumer Protection Act 2019 protects buyers of goods and services. Key provisions: District Commission handles claims up to ₹1 crore. State Commission handles ₹1–10 crore. National Commission handles above ₹10 crore. You can file complaint online at edaakhil.nic.in. Product liability has been introduced — manufacturers are liable for defects.

---

Q: What are my rights as a consumer?
A: Six Consumer Rights under Indian law: 1. Right to Safety — protection from hazardous goods 2. Right to be Informed — accurate information about products 3. Right to Choose — access to variety at competitive prices 4. Right to be Heard — your complaints must be addressed 5. Right to Redress — get compensation for defective products 6. Right to Consumer Education — awareness about rights

---

Q: What is deficiency of service?
A: Deficiency of service means any fault, imperfection, shortcoming or inadequacy in the quality, nature and manner of performance which is required to be maintained under any law or contract. Examples: doctor negligence, bank errors, insurance claim rejection, builder delays, poor hotel service. You can file a consumer complaint for all these.

---

Q: How to file RTI application?
A: **RTI Application Process:**
1. Write application to Public Information Officer (PIO) of the concerned department
2. Pay fee: ₹10 for Central Govt departments
3. Send by registered post or hand delivery
4. Response must come within 30 days (48 hours for life/liberty matters)
5. First Appeal to Appellate Authority within 30 days if unsatisfied
6. Second Appeal to Information Commission within 90 days
File online: rtionline.gov.in

---

Q: What is RTI?
A: RTI stands for Right to Information Act 2005. It gives every Indian citizen the right to request information from any government body. The government must reply within 30 days. RTI fee is just ₹10. Below Poverty Line citizens pay no fee. You can get: copies of documents, inspection of records, certified copies of government files. RTI cannot be used for personal information about private individuals.
